Just 30 Seconds Privacy Policy

equal (the developer) values the privacy of users of Just 30 Seconds (하루 30초). This policy explains what information the app processes and how it protects that information.

1. Information processed

The app stores the following information on your device: onboarding completion status, sentences you write, the dates and times when you complete 30-second sessions, attendance dates, and the number of sessions.

The app does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers.

2. Purposes of processing

Stored information is used only to display your sentences again and provide session and attendance records.

3. External transfers and third-party sharing

The current version does not use accounts, advertising SDKs, external analytics SDKs, or a developer-operated server. Sentences entered in the app and usage records are not transmitted externally or shared with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

Information is retained as app data on your device. You can delete it at any time using Delete all data in the app's App information and settings screen, or Android's clear app data function. When you uninstall the app, local data is also deleted according to the operating system's policies.

App data is excluded from Android automatic backup and device transfer.

5. Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Additional applicable policies will be applied according to the final target age groups specified on Google Play.

6. Changes to this policy

If app features or data handling practices change, this policy will be updated and its effective date revised.
